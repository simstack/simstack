simstack.runner_runtime.native_observations module¶

Durable native-worker ownership that survives the launching Runner.

A unique, inherited OS lease identifies the process lifetime; a recycled PID cannot acquire it. The intent is durable before spawn, and the worker keeps the lease after its separate launch reservation is released at start acknowledgement. Recovery never signals a PID or invents a lost waitpid exit status.
